## The symptom pattern

The mower is running normally — the motor sounds right and it's clearly
cutting the grass — but clippings are ending up on the lawn instead of in
the collection box, or the box is filling far less than it used to for the
same size of cut. Flymo's own manual for the Chevron 32 treats this as its
own troubleshooting item, separate from the mower failing to start or
running rough.

## What's actually happening

A lawnmower's collection box fills because the spinning blade generates an
airflow that carries cut grass up through the discharge chute and into the
box. That airflow runs through the air intakes on the deck, across the
underside of the deck itself, and out through the chute — and the manual's
own first fix for poor collection is cleaning all three. A mower can have a
perfectly sharp, correctly fitted blade and still stop collecting well if
clippings, mud or grass have built up anywhere along that path, because
it's the air movement that's been choked, not the cut.

The manual's second cause is simpler: cutting too low for how long or wet
the grass is. Raising the cutting height gives the airflow less material
to lift per pass, which is often enough on its own.

## Working through it

Unplug the mower from the mains before doing any of this — the blade can
still turn if it's switched on by accident while your hands are near the
deck. Clean the outside of the air intakes, the discharge chute, and the
underside of the deck; this is the manual's first and most direct fix, and
it costs nothing but a scraper and a few minutes.

Check the cutting height against the actual length of the grass. If you've
been cutting low or the grass has grown long or is damp, raise the height
a notch — the manual gives this as a direct cause of poor collection, not
just a general mowing tip.

If collection is still poor once the intakes, chute and deck are clean and
the height is right for the grass, the manual doesn't give a further
home-repair step for this fault — it routes anything beyond that to a
Husqvarna UK approved service centre or a specialist dealer approved by
Flymo.

## Verdict

Every cause Flymo's own table gives for this is something to clean or
adjust, not something to buy or replace — there's no part this fault needs.
Work through the intakes and chute first, since caked-on buildup there is
the most common reason a mower that's still cutting properly stops
collecting well, then check the cutting height before assuming anything's
actually broken. If none of that brings collection back, that's the
manual's own line for booking it in rather than continuing to take it
apart.
