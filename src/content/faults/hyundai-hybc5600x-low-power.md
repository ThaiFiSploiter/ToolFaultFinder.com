## The symptom pattern

The brush cutter starts without a fight, but once it's running it won't
pick up to full power — it sounds flat or labours rather than revving
cleanly. Hyundai's troubleshooting table for this machine treats that as
its own listed fault, separate from the engine not starting at all and
separate from it running roughly once started.

## What's actually happening

The table names two causes. The first is an incorrectly adjusted choke
lever — the manual's own fix for it, stated plainly in the table, is to
move the lever to the upper position. That's the same position used to
start the engine from cold, which the manual separately says then
disengages on its own, back to the off position, once you squeeze the
throttle after starting.

The second is more straightforward: this machine uses an oil-soaked foam
air filter, and the manual is explicit that a dirty one restricts airflow
to the carburettor and reduces power and efficiency. It's also the cause
with a maintenance schedule behind it — Hyundai calls for inspecting the
filter every 25 hours of use, and more often in dusty conditions, so a
filter that's gone past due is a genuinely ordinary explanation rather than
a stretch.

## Working through it

Let the engine cool before touching anything, then try the table's own
fix for this symptom directly: move the choke lever to the upper position
and see if full power returns. That's the literal fix the manual gives for
an "incorrectly adjusted" choke lever on this exact fault — it doesn't
explain further, so there's nothing more specific to check beyond trying
it.

Then deal with the air filter regardless, since it costs nothing to check:
unclip the air filter housing cover, pull the foam element out, and look
at it. Clean it by tapping or blowing the debris out, then re-soak it in
oil before it goes back in — the manual is specific that the filter must
be refitted oiled, and that running the machine without it fitted at all
can damage the engine in a way the warranty doesn't cover. Refit the cover
and test.

If the engine still won't reach full power after both checks, this
particular table row has nothing further to offer. The manual's separate
row for rough or uneven running points at the spark plug and carburettor
adjustment instead — a different symptom — and carburettor adjustment is
the one item in that row routed to a service dealer rather than given as
a home procedure.

## Verdict

Neither named cause costs anything to rule out, and the air filter in
particular is exactly the kind of maintenance item that's easy to let slide
on a machine used occasionally through a summer. Try the choke lever and
pull the filter before assuming anything is actually wrong with the
engine.
