## The symptom pattern

Every mower has some vibration, but this is different — a noticeably
heavier, more intense shake than normal, strong enough to feel through
the handlebar on every pass. Einhell's own troubleshooting table for the
GC-PM 52/2 S HW (shared with its 47/2 and 56/2 siblings) names this
exactly: "the equipment does not operate smoothly and vibrates
intensively," with three possible causes.

## What Einhell's own table says

Section 9 gives the causes in this order: bolts loose somewhere on the
machine, the blade attachment itself loose, or the blade imbalanced. The
manual opens the whole troubleshooting section with a safety warning that
applies to all three: switch off the engine and pull out the spark plug
boot before making any checks or adjustments, and be aware the exhaust
and other parts can still be hot for a few minutes after it's been
running.

The first two causes are genuinely a tightening job — work over the
machine for anything loose, then confirm the blade itself is seated and
fastened correctly. The third is where Einhell's guidance gets more
specific than the fault table's one-line remedy suggests. Section 7.2.2
of the same manual states plainly: "for safety reasons, have all blade
sharpening, balancing and mounting work carried out by an authorized
service center." It also gives the method for checking a blade after it's
hit something — "tilt the lawn mower to the rear" — and is direct about
damaged blades: "damaged or bent blades must be replaced. Never straighten
a bent blade. Never work with bent or heavily worn blades as this will
result in vibrations, causing further damage to the mower."

## Working through it

With the engine off and the spark plug lead pulled, check the whole
machine for loose bolts first, then the blade's own fixing. If both are
tight, tilt the mower back and inspect the blade itself for bends, chips
or uneven wear — a blade that's visibly fine but the mower still
vibrates heavily is the imbalance case Einhell points to a service
centre for, not a DIY rebalancing job.

## Verdict

Loose bolts and a loose blade fixing are free, five-minute fixes and
probably the more common of the three causes on a mower that's seen
regular use. A bent or damaged blade needs replacing rather than
straightening, by Einhell's own instruction. Where it comes down to the
blade's balance rather than visible damage, Einhell's own manual routes
the sharpening, balancing and mounting work to an authorized service
centre — worth paying for rather than doing yourself, since a spinning
blade that isn't properly balanced or seated is a safety issue, not just
a performance one, and the manual says so in its own words.
