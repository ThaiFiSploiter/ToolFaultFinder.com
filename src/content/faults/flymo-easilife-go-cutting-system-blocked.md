## The symptom pattern

The EasiLife GO stops partway through a mowing cycle and the Flymo app
(or the mower itself) reports **"Cutting system blocked."** It's one of
a long list of named fault messages in the manual's own troubleshooting
chapter, each with its own row of causes — this one is specifically
about the blade disc, not the drive wheels, the charging station, or the
boundary-wire signal, which are separate rows with separate causes.

## What's actually happening

Flymo's manual gives this exact message three causes, and all three are
physical rather than anything electronic:

- Grass or another object is wrapped or caught around the blade disc.
- The blade disc is sitting in standing water.
- The grass in the work area is taller than the mower is designed to
  cut — the manual states a hard maximum of 4cm (1.6in) before the
  product is installed.

None of these point at a fault inside the mower. They're all conditions
in the lawn or underneath the deck that stop the blade disc turning
freely, and the manual's own fix for each is to change that condition,
not to replace anything.

## Working through it

Lift the mower clear of the lawn before you do anything else, and keep
your hands away from the blade disc — treat it the same as you would
any blade, powered or not.

Check the blade disc itself first. Grass wrapped tightly around it,
moss, or anything else caught against it is the manual's first named
cause, and the fix is simply to clear it by hand with the mower lifted
and powered down.

With that ruled out, look at where the mower actually stopped. If it's
sitting in a patch that collects standing water, the manual treats that
as a separate cause from debris — the fix is to stop water pooling
there or keep the mower out of that part of the work area, not anything
done to the mower.

Finally, check the grass height generally. Flymo's limit is explicit:
4cm (1.6in) maximum before the mower goes to work. If the lawn's grown
past that, the manual is clear that's the cause, not a mower fault — cut
it down with a conventional mower first, then let the EasiLife GO take
over once the lawn is back within its stated limit.

## Verdict

This fault message is entirely about conditions around the blade disc,
not a failure in the mower's electronics or drive system. Work through
the three causes in the order the manual gives them — debris at the
disc, standing water, grass height — since all three cost nothing and
need no part to fix. The manual's only escalation point for this
specific message is persistence: if "Cutting system blocked" keeps
recurring after all three are genuinely ruled out, that's when it says
to speak to Flymo service, not before.
